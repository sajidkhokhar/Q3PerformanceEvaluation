02 — App Plans

The detail behind each focus

Click a card to expand. Left: what's changing. Right: what's broken today vs. the target.

01 Snore Tracker Autonomous ★ 4.5 · 298 ▾
Q4 actions
  • Re-run ASO keyword research (snore, snoring sounds, sleep apnea detector) and refresh screenshots around the sound-analysis dashboard
  • Map the onboarding funnel step by step and fix the single biggest drop-off point first
  • Move the rating prompt to a positive moment (good-sleep result), gated so unhappy users go to feedback instead
  • Run premium A/B tests across pricing (trial length), design (paywall layout) and functionality (which feature unlocks first)
⚠ What's broken today
  • Named directly against a competitor: "Download Snore Lab instead... not accurate"
  • A 1★ review reports recording fails ~50% of nights
  • No Apple Health integration — called "outdated," no AI insight
  • Happy users blocked from replaying recordings without paying
✦ Q4 target
  • Detection accuracy verified against a known test set
  • Onboarding completion at 90%
  • Apple Health integration shipped as a listing differentiator
  • Premium playback tested as an A/B variant, not just gated

Source: public App Store listing & customer reviews, pulled live Oct 7, 2026.

02 Video to Live Guided ★ 4.5 · 4,245 ▾
Q4 actions
  • Ship a build this quarter — fix the video-orientation and auto-filter bugs first
  • Confirm whether reviews are currently incentivized by a premium unlock, and remove it if so
  • Cap paywall/subscription-prompt frequency per session
  • Run premium A/B tests (pricing, paywall placement, hook feature) once trust issues are fixed

  • Orientation bug squishes/flips Live Photos — reported independently 3×
  • Auto color filter can't be reverted to the original
  • Subscription popups called "so annoying" in multiple reviews
✦ Q4 target
  • First shipped build in 4 years
  • Orientation fixed + "original color" toggle added
  • No incentivized-review mechanic in the current flow
  • Purchase count lifted via A/B-tested premium flow

03 Menu Smart Guided ▾
Q4 actions
  • Build Android to feature + design parity with iOS — no scope creep before launch
  • Build the Play Store listing in parallel: feature graphic, screenshots, keyword parity
  • Daily iOS analytics check — installs, funnel, crash rate, new reviews — while the launch is fresh

  • App Store rating held at ≥ 4.7★ through the launch window
04 Translate AI Guided ★ 4.4 · 328 ▾
Q4 actions
  • Add a copy button to the translation result screen
  • Test speech-to-text accuracy/config — likely root cause, not the translation engine
  • Surface the language picker on the main screen instead of burying it
  • Show remaining free translations before the trial limit hits, not after
  • Standardize the store title/listing copy across the India and US storefronts
⚠ What's broken today
  • Can't copy translated text — repeated across multiple reviews, blocks real use
  • Voice recognition misunderstands short spoken phrases
  • Users think only Spanish is supported — picker isn't discoverable
  • Trial limit feels like a "scam" when it hits with no warning
✦ Q4 target
  • Copy-to-clipboard shipped on the result screen
  • Rating trend tracked weekly after the first fix release
  • Store page reflects the rebuilt app's real feature set

04 — Operating Rhythm

The cadence underneath every app

What keeps gaps and drops from sitting unnoticed for a month.

Daily

  • Installs, crashes, funnel & new reviews across all 5 apps
  • Extra depth on Menu Smart iOS during its launch window
  • Log anomalies same-day, not batched for the weekly review

Weekly

  • Keyword rank check for Snore Tracker & Video to Live
  • Review A/B test readouts; kill or extend variants
  • Triage and respond to new store reviews

Monthly

  • Full funnel/cohort review per app
  • Decide which A/B variants roll out to 100%
  • Rating-trend report; re-prioritize next month

End of quarter

  • Compile ASO performance + rating movement
  • Confirm Android (Menu Smart) launch status
  • Draft Q1 2027 priorities from what's still open
