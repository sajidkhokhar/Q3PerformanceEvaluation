What real reviews say right now — fix list

"Download SNORE LAB instead"★★☆☆☆

"Not accurate. My snoring wakes my wife up at night... my wife would make note of the times and it didn't match." — the #2 most-helpful review on the listing, naming a competitor directly.

Fix: audit detection accuracy against a known-snoring test set before any ASO push — a named-competitor comparison this visible undercuts every install it earns.
"Not worthwhile"★☆☆☆☆

"App failed to record 50% of nights. Otherwise fine, good even, but that's a high failure rate. Also, a subscription for an app that entirely lives in..."

Fix: background-recording reliability (iOS backgrounding/mic session interruptions) is likely the actual onboarding-drop-off culprit, not just the permission screen.
"Major disappointment"★☆☆☆☆

"The data feels very inaccurate, it does not integrate with Apple Health, and the app feels outdated with no meaningful AI or useful sleep insight."

Fix: Apple Health integration is a free, high-signal differentiator — add it as a listing talking point once shipped, not just a changelog line.
Recordings paywalled★★★★★

"Super helpful to know if I'm snoring since I sleep by myself. I would like to be able to listen to the recordings without paying.."

Fix: a happy user blocked by the paywall is exactly who the premium A/B tests below should target — test unlocking playback vs. keeping it gated.

ASO — keyword & listing

  • Re-run keyword research around snore, snoring sounds, sleep tracker, sleep apnea detector; lead title/subtitle with the highest-intent term
  • Refresh screenshots to show the sound-analysis dashboard and the premium insights screen, not just the record button
  • Add/update preview video; A/B the icon if rank plateaus after metadata refresh
  • Watch for the Jan "New Year health" search spike — have metadata locked by mid-Dec

Onboarding & drop-off repair

  • Instrument funnel step-by-step (permissions → account → first sleep session setup) if not already split out in analytics
  • Identify the single biggest drop-off step first — fix that before touching the rest of the flow
  • Re-test permission-request timing/copy (mic/notifications) — usually the top drop point in sleep apps
  • Re-measure weekly against the 90% target, not just at quarter-end

Rating & review strategy

  • Move the rating prompt to a positive moment (e.g. a good-sleep-night result or a completed week of tracking), not right after onboarding
  • Gate it: ask "Enjoying Snore Tracker?" first — happy users go to the store prompt, unhappy users go to an in-app feedback form instead
  • Reply to recent store reviews that flag bugs or confusion, especially around premium

Real finding: the live build is version 1.4, released 11 Dec 2022 — the app has collected 4,245 ratings since on a nearly 4-year-old binary. Every bug below has been live the entire time.
Why now: acquisition is healthy — the gap is trust signals. Daily users are up but ratings/reviews lag, which caps future organic growth even if installs keep climbing.

What real reviews say right now — fix list

"Bad editor" / "Needs work" / "Yup"★☆☆☆☆

"Turns videos to Live Photos but it turns the video to the side and squishes it making it look bad" — this orientation bug is reported independently across at least 3 separate reviews.

Fix: top priority bug for Q4 — portrait/landscape handling in the video→Live Photo conversion pipeline. Recurring, specific, and easy to reproduce from the reports alone.
Unwanted auto color filter★★☆☆☆

"The app automatically distorts the colors of the Live Photo and you can't change it back to iPhone's original color format."

Fix: add a "no filter / original" toggle as the processing default, not an opt-in buried in settings.
Subscription prompt fatigue★☆☆☆☆

"The popups are so annoying please get rid of them I can't stand it" / "keeps popping up asking me to buy subscription."

Fix: paywall frequency/timing is exactly what the premium A/B design axis below should resolve — cap prompt frequency per session as a quick win first.
Reviews written "to get premium"★★★★★

Multiple top reviews read like: "I'm only writing this to get premium" / "writing a 100 word review for premium so why not" — a recurring pattern among the most-visible 5★ reviews.

Fix — compliance risk: if reviews are currently incentivized by a premium unlock, that violates App Store guideline 4.1 (incentivized reviews) and risks removal. Confirm whether this exists in the current flow and remove it before the rating/review strategy below goes live.

ASO — protect, don't disrupt

  • No title/keyword changes while rankings are healthy — track weekly instead of iterating blind
  • Refresh screenshots/preview video seasonally with real before/after transformations and UGC, since the creative is what can move rating perception pre-download

Flow, design & bug fixes

  • Full bug sweep across the export/share flow — this is usually where a "great idea, rough execution" review comes from
  • Pass over overall UI/UX for friction points between "upload video" and "see live photo result"
  • Re-check performance on older/low-end devices — a common silent source of 2–3★ reviews in media-processing apps

Rating & review strategy

  • Trigger the rating prompt right after a successful export/share — the app's clearest "delight" moment
  • Use a pre-prompt gate ("Enjoying your live photo?") to route unhappy users to private feedback instead of the store
  • Respond publicly to existing reviews that cite bugs once fixes ship — shows active development to new visitors reading reviews

Menu Smart

Guided
Backup: Sourabh
Q4 focus
Android parity + iOS launch monitoring
Primary KPI
Hold App Store rating ≥ 4.7★
Secondary KPI
User traffic, Android build progress
Risk
New iOS launch is fragile — daily analytics review is non-negotiable this quarter
Why now: iOS just launched and is being judged in real time by new users; Android doesn't exist yet. Both compete for the same design/eng attention, so sequence matters — iOS stability first, Android build in parallel without slowing iOS fixes.

Android build — parity with iOS

  • Match iOS feature set and visual design 1:1 for launch — no scope creep, parity first, platform-specific polish later
  • Build Play Store listing in parallel: feature graphic, screenshots sized for Android devices, localized metadata matched to iOS keyword strategy
  • Closed beta in November, soft launch in December once core flows are stable

iOS — daily analytics & journey check

  • Daily check: installs, funnel completion, crash rate, new reviews — early weeks of a launch are when drop-off patterns are most visible and most fixable
  • Map the user journey from install → first meal plan/recipe generated; find and fix the biggest gap first
  • Feed every finding into the fix backlog the same week, not batched monthly

Rating & review strategy — hold 4.7★+

  • Trigger rating prompt after a clear success moment (meal plan created / recipe saved), gated behind a satisfaction check
  • Respond to every new iOS review during the launch window — both to catch bugs early and to signal active support to browsers
  • Once Android ships, apply the same prompt timing and response cadence there from day one rather than retrofitting later

Risk

Real finding: the India and US store listings use inconsistent titles — "Translate AI - Live Translator" vs. "Translate AI- Voice Translator" (missing space before the dash). A free, same-day fix.
Why now: the app was rebuilt and the old issues are fixed, but the store page doesn't know that — old negative reviews are still the first thing visitors read, and the new version isn't yet earning enough positive reviews to outweigh them.

What real reviews say right now — fix list

Can't copy translated text★☆☆☆☆

"Why would you not allow the user to copy the translated text from the app to a chat to be used to communicate with a person" — reported independently in at least two separate reviews.

Fix: this is the single most repeated, most fixable complaint — add a copy button to the result screen. Blocks the app's core real-world use case (translate, then send).
Voice translation accuracy★☆☆☆☆

"Works poorly for voice; not ready for use... most of the time doesn't understand spoken two or three word sentences. Those it does understand, it gets wrong."

Fix: speech-to-text accuracy, not the translation engine itself, is the likely root cause — test the STT provider/config before touching translation logic.
"What are they thinking....."★☆☆☆☆

"You type in some text and it translates it into Spanish with no options to translate it into any other language."

Fix: this reads like a discoverability bug, not a missing feature — the language picker is likely too hidden. Surface it on the main screen.
"Scammed me"★☆☆☆☆

"It seemed to work OK and got pretty much everything right, but I hit my daily limit for the trial, so I..." — felt deceived rather than informed about the limit.

Fix: show remaining free translations before the limit hits, not after — a messaging fix, not a pricing one.

Bug fixes first

  • Pull the actual complaint themes from both the old negative reviews and any new ones — fix what's still real before touching messaging
  • Re-test the core translate/conversation flow end-to-end on the current build

Listing — reset expectations

  • Update screenshots and description to reflect the rebuilt app and current feature set — mismatched expectations are a common cause of the exact negative reviews described here
  • Use the "What's New" field to explicitly call out the rebuild/fixes on every release this quarter

Rating & review recovery strategy

  • Add an in-app rating prompt after a successful translation/conversation session, gated so dissatisfied users go to feedback, not the store
  • Publicly respond to recent App Store reviews, acknowledging old issues and pointing to what's fixed — new visitors read replies, not just star counts
  • Track rating trend weekly (not monthly) after the first fix release ships, so the strategy can be adjusted mid-quarter instead of at the end
